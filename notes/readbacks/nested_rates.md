## 1. `mimc_antithetic_quadratic`

**Rendering.** Let $f(x)=c_0+c_1x+\frac K2x^2$ for all real $x$, and let $G,a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2\in\mathbb R$. Write $D_G(a,b)=f(G+\frac{a+b}2)-\frac12f(G+a)-\frac12f(G+b)$. The theorem claims two things:
- (i) $D_G(a_1,b_1)-D_G(a_2,b_2)=-\frac K8\big((a_1-b_1)^2-(a_2-b_2)^2\big)$;
- (ii) if $K\neq0$ and $(a_1-b_1)^2\ne(a_2-b_2)^2$, then the same difference is $\neq-\frac K4\big((a_1-b_1)^2-(a_2-b_2)^2\big)$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. For a quadratic, $D_G(a,b)=-\frac K8(a-b)^2$: the constant and linear parts cancel. The two candidate right-hand sides differ by $\frac K8((a_1-b_1)^2-(a_2-b_2)^2)$, which is nonzero under the premises of (ii). Checked symbolically with sympy (`t01_quadratic.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none. Take $f(x)=x^2/2$; the premises of (ii) hold at $K=1,a_1=1,b_1=a_2=b_2=0$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Standard fact and fidelity:* this is the midpoint second difference of a quadratic. Part (ii) records that the $-\tfrac14$ printed in Giles (2015, §9.2) is a misprint for $-\tfrac18$, in this normalisation.

## 2. `abs_mimc_antithetic_le`

**Rendering.** Assume $f$ is differentiable everywhere with derivative $f'$, and $f'$ is $K$-Lipschitz. Then for all $A_1,A_2,B_1,B_2$:
$$|D(A_1,A_2)-D(B_1,B_2)|\le\tfrac K8\big((A_1-A_2)^2+(B_1-B_2)^2\big).$$

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true.
  - Put $h=(y-x)/2$. Then $|f(m+h)+f(m-h)-2f(m)|=|\int_0^h(f'(m+t)-f'(m-t))dt|\le Kh^2$, so $|D(x,y)|\le\frac K8(x-y)^2$.
  - The triangle inequality over the two pairs gives the claim.
  - The bound is sharp. Take $f''=+1$ near $A$ and $-1$ near $B$ (square-wave $f''$): the ratio is exactly 1. Random search with $\sin$ and $\log\cosh$ gives sup ratio → 1 from below (`t02_pointwise.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; e.g. $f=\sin$, $K=1$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Remark:* this is the elementary midpoint-convexity defect bound for $C^{1,1}$ functions. As a MIMC estimate it is the "trivial" bound: it does not decay as $(B_1,B_2)\to(A_1,A_2)$.

## 3. `abs_mimc_antithetic_taylor_le`

**Rendering.** Assume $f$ is twice differentiable ($f'$ exists everywhere and has derivative $f''$), and $f''$ is $L$-Lipschitz; no bound on $f''$ itself is assumed. Then for all $G,a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2$:
$$\Big|D_G(a_1,b_1)-D_G(a_2,b_2)+\tfrac{f''(G)}8\big((a_1-b_1)^2-(a_2-b_2)^2\big)\Big|\le\tfrac L{16}\,\mathcal M^2\big(|\Delta a+\Delta b|+|\Delta a-\Delta b|\big)+\tfrac L8\,\mathcal S\,\mathcal M\,|\Delta a-\Delta b|,$$
where:
- $\mathcal M=\max(|a_1-b_1|,|a_2-b_2|)$, and it is $\mathcal M$ that is squared: `max … ^ 2` $=(\max…)^2$, checked;
- $\mathcal S=\max(|a_1+b_1|,|a_2+b_2|)$;
- $\Delta a=a_1-a_2$ and $\Delta b=b_1-b_2$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true, with exactly this constant.
  - Put $s=\frac{a+b}2$, $d=\frac{a-b}2$ and $h(x)=f''(G+x)-f''(G)$, so $|h(x)|\le L|x|$ and $h$ is $L$-Lipschitz.
  - Taylor with integral remainder gives $D_G+\frac{f''(G)}2d^2=-\frac{d^2}2\Psi(s,d)$, where $\Psi=\int_0^1(1-u)[h(s+du)+h(s-du)]du$.
  - Hence $|\Psi|\le L(|s|+|d|/3)$ and $|\Psi_1-\Psi_2|\le L(|\Delta s|+|\Delta d|/3)$.
  - Split $d_1^2\Psi_1-d_2^2\Psi_2=d_1^2(\Psi_1-\Psi_2)+(d_1^2-d_2^2)\Psi_2$. This gives $\frac L2D^2(|\Delta s|+|\Delta d|)+L\,S'D\,|\Delta d|$ with $D=\mathcal M/2$ and $S'=\mathcal S/2$, which is exactly the stated RHS.
- *Sharpness:* for $f=x^3/6$ the remainder is $-sd^2/2$, and equality holds exactly when $d_1=d_2$. Numerically the sup ratio → 1 from below for $x^3/6$, $\sin$, $|x|^3/6$, and $f'''$ a square wave (`t02_pointwise.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; e.g. $f=x^3/6$, $L=1$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Standard fact:* the second-order Taylor expansion of the difference of two antithetic defects (the MIMC mixed-difference expansion), with the correct coefficient $\tfrac18$ and a Lipschitz-$f''$ remainder. The RHS vanishes when $(a_1,b_1)=(a_2,b_2)$, so the bound is genuinely of mixed-difference type.

## 4. `abs_mimc_antithetic_le_of_deriv2`

**Rendering.** Assume $f\in C^2$ with $f'$ $K$-Lipschitz (so $|f''|\le K$) and $f''$ $L$-Lipschitz. Then for all $A_1,A_2,B_1,B_2$ and **every** $\mu\in\mathbb R$:
$$|D(A_1,A_2)-D(B_1,B_2)|\le\tfrac L8\mathcal M^2|\mu|+\tfrac K2\mathcal M\Big|\tfrac{(A_1-B_1)+(A_2-B_2)}2-\mu\Big|+\tfrac K4\mathcal M\,|(A_1-B_1)-(A_2-B_2)|,$$
where $\mathcal M=\max(|A_1-A_2|,|B_1-B_2|)$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. Write $D$ as $D(m,d)=f(m)-\frac{f(m+d)+f(m-d)}2$.
  - Shifting $m_A\mapsto m_A-\mu$ costs at most $\frac12d_A^2L|\mu|$, by the representation used in thm 3.
  - Along the segment from $(m_A-\mu,d_A)$ to $(m_B,d_B)$, both partial derivatives of $D$ are bounded by $K|d|\le K\max(|d_A|,|d_B|)$.
  - Adding the two gives exactly the RHS.
- *Numerics:* sup ratio 0.9995 (square-wave $f'''$) and 0.957 ($\sin$) (`t02_pointwise.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; e.g. $f=\sin$, $K=L=1$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Remark:* this is the pointwise lemma behind thm 18, used with $\mu=E[g_{\ell_2+1}-g_{\ell_2}\mid z]$. With $\mu=0$ it reduces to a plain Lipschitz bound on $D$.

## 6. `nestedSdeDelta` (definition)

**Rendering.** $\Delta_0=P_0$. For $M=2^\ell$:
$$\Delta_{\ell+1}(z,w)=f\big(\overline{g_{\ell+1}}^{[0,2M)}\big)-\tfrac12f\big(\overline{g_\ell}^{[0,M)}\big)-\tfrac12f\big(\overline{g_\ell}^{[M,2M)}\big),$$
where $\overline{g}^{I}$ is the average of $g(z,w_m)$ over $m\in I$. The second half is taken via `shiftSeq`.

**Assessment.** Faithful to the antithetic estimator with a level-dependent inner approximation (Giles 2015, §9.2). Between levels both the number of inner samples and the approximation level change. The fine and coarse terms use the same inner samples $w$, which is the coupling. It is identical to the library definition (checked with `cases ℓ <;> rfl`).

## 8. `nested_sde_variance_rate`

**Rendering.** $\nu,\rho$ are probability measures, and $f$ is differentiable with $f'$ $K$-Lipschitz. Hypotheses, for every $\ell$:
- each $g_\ell$ is jointly measurable;
- $g_\ell^4\in L^1(\nu\otimes\rho)$ and $\int g_\ell^4\,d(\nu\otimes\rho)\le m_4$ (integrated over $z$, uniform in $\ell$);
- strong $L^4$ rate of the coupled differences: $(2^\ell)^4\int(g_{\ell+1}-g_\ell)^4\,d(\nu\otimes\rho)\le c_s$, both evaluated at the same $(z,w)$.

Conclusion, for every $\ell$: $\Delta_{\ell+1}\in L^2(\nu\otimes\rho^{\otimes\mathbb N})$, and
$$4^\ell\,E[\Delta_{\ell+1}^2]\le V:=3\big(\tfrac K8\big)^2224\,m_4+\tfrac32\big(8(|f'(0)|^4+K^4m_4)+(1+\tfrac{K^2}2)c_s\big).$$
The factor `3 / 2` is the real number 1.5 (checked). So $\beta=2$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true.
  - Write $\bar A_1,\bar A_2$ for the $g_\ell$-means of the two halves, $\bar A_c=\frac{\bar A_1+\bar A_2}2$, and $\bar\delta=\bar A_f-\bar A_c$ (the mean of $g_{\ell+1}-g_\ell$ over the $2M$ samples).
  - Decompose $\Delta_{\ell+1}=f'(\bar A_c)\bar\delta+R+D(\bar A_1,\bar A_2)$, with $|R|\le\frac K2\bar\delta^2$ and $|D|\le\frac K8(\bar A_1-\bar A_2)^2$.
  - Use $(a+b+c)^2\le3(a^2+b^2+c^2)$.
  - The power-mean inequality gives $E\bar\delta^4\le E(g_{\ell+1}-g_\ell)^4\le c_s2^{-4\ell}$ and $E\bar A_c^4\le m_4$.
  - Since $|f'(x)|\le|f'(0)|+K|x|$, $Ef'(\bar A_c)^4\le8(|f'(0)|^4+K^4m_4)$.
  - Cauchy–Schwarz and AM–GM give the $\frac32(\cdots)$ term.
  - For the antithetic term, $M^2E(\bar A_1-\bar A_2)^4\le3\cdot16\,m_4=48m_4\le224m_4$.
- *Numerics:* exact expectations in random finite models give a maximum ratio of 0.103 over 3000 models (`t08_sde_mimc.out`), and 0.139 under adversarial hill-climbing (`t08b_hillclimb.out`). The targeted affine case gives 0.118, which is the AM–GM slack.
- *Vacuity:* none. Take $\nu=\rho=U[0,1]$, $g_\ell(z,w)=z+w+2^{-\ell}$, $f=\sin$: then $m_4=81$ and $c_s=1/16$.
- *Junk values:* none. `MemLp` is asserted, and the integrals in the hypotheses are genuine because $g_\ell^4$ is integrable.
- *Remarks:* the bound uses raw moments and $|f'(0)|$, so it is not translation-invariant; it is valid but loose. Matches the paper's $\beta=2$.

## 10. `nested_sde_bias_rate`

**Rendering.** Hypotheses:
- $f$ is differentiable with $f'$ $K$-Lipschitz;
- $g_\ell$ are measurable with the $L^4$ bound $m_4$ as in 8 ($c_s$ is not used);
- the limit $g$ is jointly measurable with $g^4\in L^1(\nu\otimes\rho)$;
- uniform weak rate: for every $\ell$ and ν-a.e. $z$, $2^\ell\,|E_\rho g_\ell(z,\cdot)-m(z)|\le c_w$.

Conclusion, for every $\ell$:
$$2^\ell\,\big|E[P_\ell]-E_\nu f(m(Z))\big|\le\tfrac K2(1+16m_4)+c_w\!\int|f'(m(z))|\,\nu(dz)+\tfrac K2c_w^2.$$

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true.
  - Taylor at $m(z)$: $f(\bar A_\ell)-f(m)=f'(m)(\bar A_\ell-m)+R$ with $|R|\le\frac K2(\bar A_\ell-m)^2$.
  - $E[\bar A_\ell-m\mid z]=m_\ell(z)-m(z)$, where $m_\ell(z)$ is the conditional mean of $g_\ell$, and its absolute value is at most $c_w2^{-\ell}$.
  - $2^\ell E(\bar A_\ell-m)^2\le Eg_\ell^2+c_w^2\le\sqrt{m_4}+c_w^2\le1+16m_4+c_w^2$.
- *The integral $\int|f'(m)|d\nu$ is genuine:* $f'\circ m$ is measurable, and $|f'(m)|\le|f'(0)|+K|m|$ with $m\in L^4(\nu)$. So there is no junk 0.
- *Sharpness:* for affine $f$ ($K=0$) and constant $E[h\mid z]$, equality holds (ratio 1 up to $3\cdot10^{-14}$). The maximum ratio with $K>0$ is 0.9925 (`t08_sde_mimc.out`). The $c_w$ term therefore cannot be improved.
- *Hypotheses:* `hw` is uniform in $z$ (an ess sup), stronger than needed, but jointly satisfiable with `hm₄` and `hs`: in the example of 8, $c_w=1$.
- *Vacuity / junk values:* not vacuous (example of 8); no junk values.
- *Standard fact:* bias $O(1/M)$ from inner sampling plus the $O(2^{-\ell})$ weak error of $g_\ell$, i.e. $\alpha=1$ as in the paper.

## 11. `nested_sde_mlmc_complexity`

**Rendering.** Hypotheses:
- all hypotheses of 8 and 10;
- i.i.d. samples: $\omega_{(\ell,n)}:\Omega\to\mathcal Z\times\mathcal W^{\mathbb N}$, each with law $\nu\otimes\rho^{\otimes\mathbb N}$ (`MeasurePreserving`), and mutually independent over $\mathbb N\times\mathbb N$ (`iIndepFun`);
- integrable costs $\text{cost}_{\ell,n}$ with mean $C_\ell\le c_3 4^\ell$, where $c_3>0$.

Conclusion: $\exists c_4>0$ (it may depend on all data, but not on $\varepsilon$) such that $\forall\varepsilon\in(0,e^{-1})$ $\exists L\in\mathbb N$ and $N:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ with all $N_\ell\ge1$, satisfying:
- $E_\mu\Big[\Big(\sum_{\ell=0}^L\frac1{N_\ell}\sum_{n<N_\ell}\Delta_\ell(\omega_{\ell,n})-E_\nu f(m(Z))\Big)^2\Big]<\varepsilon^2$;
- $E_\mu\big[\sum_{\ell\le L}\sum_{n<N_\ell}\text{cost}_{\ell,n}\big]\le c_4\,\varepsilon^{-2}(\log\varepsilon)^2$, where $\varepsilon^{-2}$ is `rpow`.

The target is subtracted outside the sum (checked by `rfl`).

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. This is the standard MLMC complexity theorem (Giles 2008, Thm 3.1; 2015, Thm 1) with:
  - $\alpha=1$, from thms 10 and 7 (telescoping);
  - $\beta=2$, from thm 8, and $V_0<\infty$ because $f$ has quadratic growth and $g_0\in L^4$;
  - $\gamma=2$.

  Since $\alpha\ge\frac12\min(\beta,\gamma)$ with $\beta=\gamma$, the cost is $\varepsilon^{-2}\log^2\varepsilon$.
- *The squared error is a genuine integral:* each $\Delta_\ell\in L^2$ and the $\omega$'s are measure-preserving, so it is not a junk 0.
- *Numerics:* the standard $(L,N)$ recipe gives cost$/(\varepsilon^{-2}\log^2\varepsilon)$ between 106 and 653, bounded, for $\varepsilon$ down to $10^{-7}$ (`t07_t11_telescoping_complexity.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; example of 8, with $\Omega$ the infinite product and $\omega$ the coordinates, and cost $\equiv4^\ell$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Remarks:* the cost model is abstract; negative $C_\ell$ only helps. Fidelity: $\alpha=1,\beta=2,\gamma=2$ and $O(\varepsilon^{-2}\log^2\varepsilon)$, as in the context.

## 12. `antithetic_kink`

**Rendering.** Let $f(x)=a_0+a_1x+c\max(x-k,0)$. For all $A_1,A_2$:
- (i) if $(A_1-k)(A_2-k)\ge0$, then $D(A_1,A_2)=0$;
- (ii) $|D(A_1,A_2)|\le\frac{|c|}4|A_1-A_2|$;
- (iii) for all $d$, $|D(k-d,k+d)|=\frac{|c|}4|2d|$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. $f$ is affine on each side of $k$. For samples that straddle $k$, $|D|=\frac{|c|}2\min(|A_1-k|,|A_2-k|)\le\frac{|c|}4|A_1-A_2|$. Part (iii) shows that (ii) is attained.
- *Numerics:* 200 000 exact rational tests, with no violation and maximum ratio 1 (`t01_quadratic.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; e.g. $f(x)=\max(x,0)$.
- *Junk values:* none.

## 13. `nested_kink_variance_rate`

**Rendering.** $f$ is piecewise linear with a kink at $k$ and slope jump $c$. The inner function is the exact $g$ (no $g_\ell$), jointly measurable. Hypotheses:
- `hfib`: for ν-a.e. $z$, $g(z,\cdot)^4\in L^1(\rho)$ and $E_\rho g(z,\cdot)^4\le m_4$;
- `hball`: for all $t>0$, $\nu\{z:|m(z)-k|\le t\}\le\mathrm{ofReal}(c_dt)$.

Conclusion, for every $\ell$: the `nestedDelta` of level $\ell+1$ is in $L^2$, and
$$2^{1.5\ell}\,E[\Delta_{\ell+1}^2]\le2c_dc^2\sqrt{8m_4(1+16m_4)},$$
where $2^{1.5\ell}$ is `rpow` with a real exponent. So $\beta=\tfrac32$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true.
  - $|\Delta|=\frac{|c|}2\min(|\bar A_1-k|,|\bar A_2-k|)\mathbf 1\{\text{straddle}\}$. With $u=m(z)-k$, $E[\Delta^2\mid z]\le\frac{c^2}4\phi(|u|)$ for $\phi(u)=\min\big(\frac{\sigma^2}{2M},\frac{2EX^4}{u^2}\big)$, which is decreasing.
  - Layer-cake together with the small-ball bound gives $E\Delta^2\le\frac{c^2}4c_d\int_0^\infty\phi\le4\sqrt3\,c_dc^2m_4^{3/4}M^{-3/2}$.
  - AM–GM gives $2\sqrt{8m_4(1+16m_4)}\ge16m_4^{3/4}$, which closes the gap.
- *Numerics:* maximum ratio 0.0030 over several families: uniform $m(Z)$, atomic outer laws, inner laws depending on $z$, a Rademacher grid, and hill-climbing (`t13_kink.out`). The constant is valid but extremely loose. It improves on the elementary $\beta=1$ bound only for $M>2048c_d^2(1+16m_4)$.
- *Hypotheses satisfiable together:* $\nu=U[0,1]$, $\rho$ Rademacher, $g=z+w$, $k=\tfrac12$ give $c_d=2$ and $m_4=8$.
- *Hypotheses stronger than usual:* `hfib` bounds raw moments uniformly in $z$. This forces $|m(z)|\le m_4^{1/4}$ a.e., which excludes unbounded conditional means; the usual assumption bounds centred moments. The bound is also not translation-invariant. `hball` forces $c_d>0$.
- *Junk values:* none. $m_4\ge0$ is forced, so the argument of $\sqrt{\ }$ is nonnegative, and `MemLp` is asserted.
- *Fidelity:* matches "piecewise linear $f$ gives $\beta=1.5$".

## 14. `nested_kink_variance_rate_of_density`

**Rendering.** As 13, but `hball` is replaced by a local density bound. For some $r>0$, every measurable $s\subseteq[k-r,k+r]$ satisfies $\nu(m^{-1}(s))\le\mathrm{ofReal}(\rho_{\max})\cdot\lambda(s)$, where $\lambda$ is Lebesgue measure (`volume`). The conclusion is that of 13 with $c_d$ replaced by $\max(2\rho_{\max},1/r)$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true, by reduction to 13. For $t\le r$, $\nu\{|m-k|\le t\}\le2\rho_{\max}t$. For $t>r$ it is $\le1\le t/r$. A negative $\rho_{\max}$ is harmless: `ofReal` gives 0, and the $1/r$ branch remains. The reduction was checked numerically (`t13_kink.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; the example of 13 with $r=\tfrac12$, $\rho_{\max}=1$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Hypotheses:* the same `hfib` caveat as 13.

## 15. `nested_kink_bias_rate`

**Rendering.** $f$ kinked, $g$ measurable, `hfib` (no small-ball hypothesis). Conclusion, for every $\ell$:
$$2^{\ell/2}\,\big|E[f(\bar A_\ell)]-E_\nu f(m(Z))\big|\le(|a_1|+|c|)(1+8m_4),$$
where $\bar A_\ell$ is the mean of $2^\ell$ exact inner samples.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. $f$ is $(|a_1|+|c|)$-Lipschitz, and $E|\bar A_\ell-m|\le\sqrt{E\,\mathrm{Var}(g\mid z)/2^\ell}\le m_4^{1/4}2^{-\ell/2}\le(1+8m_4)2^{-\ell/2}$.
- *Numerics:* maximum ratio 0.149 (`t13_kink.out`).
- *Weaker than standard:* only $\alpha=\tfrac12$. The standard result under a density or small-ball assumption is $\alpha=1$. Under 15's own hypotheses, however, $\tfrac12$ is sharp: with $m(z)\equiv k$ and $\pm0.5$ inner noise, $2^{\ell/2}\cdot$bias tends to $0.1995$ (`t13_kink.out`).
- *Remark:* the $|a_1|$ term is superfluous.
- *Vacuity / junk values:* not vacuous; no junk values.

## 16. `nested_kink_mlmc_complexity`

**Rendering.** Hypotheses of 13 ($f$ kinked, $g$, `hfib`, `hball`), plus i.i.d. samples $\omega$ as in 11 and costs with $C_\ell\le c_32^\ell$. Conclusion: $\exists c_4>0\ \forall\varepsilon\in(0,e^{-1})\ \exists L,N$ with all $N_\ell\ge1$ such that the MSE against $E_\nu f(m(Z))$ is $<\varepsilon^2$ and the expected cost is $\le c_4\varepsilon^{-2}$.

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. This is the MLMC theorem with:
  - $\alpha=\tfrac12$ (thm 15);
  - $\beta=\tfrac32$ (thm 13), and $V_0<\infty$ because $f$ is Lipschitz and $g\in L^4$;
  - $\gamma=1$.

  Since $\beta>\gamma$ and $\alpha=\tfrac12\min(\beta,\gamma)$ (the boundary case), $2^L\asymp\varepsilon^{-2}$ and $\sum_{\ell\le L}C_\ell=O(\varepsilon^{-2})$, so the total cost is $O(\varepsilon^{-2})$.
- *Genuine integrals:* the target integral is genuine, since $m$ is bounded a.e.
- *Numerics:* cost$/\varepsilon^{-2}$ between 602 and 975, bounded (`t07_t11_telescoping_complexity.out`).
- *Vacuity:* none; example of 13 with product $\Omega$ and cost $\equiv2^\ell$.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Fidelity:* matches "piecewise linear $f$: cost $O(\varepsilon^{-2})$" for exact $g$ with $\gamma=1$.

## 18. `nested_mimc_variance_rate`

**Rendering.** Hypotheses:
- $f\in C^2$ with $f'$ $K$-Lipschitz and $f''$ $L$-Lipschitz;
- $g_\ell$ measurable, $Eg_\ell^4\le m_4$, and strong rate $c_s$ as in 8;
- weak rate of successive differences, uniform in $z$: for every $\ell$ and ν-a.e. $z$, $2^\ell|E_\rho(g_{\ell+1}-g_\ell)(z,\cdot)|\le c_w$.

Conclusion, for all $\ell_1,\ell_2$: $\Delta_{\ell_1+1,\ell_2+1}\in L^2$, and
$$4^{\ell_1+\ell_2}\,E[\Delta^2_{\ell_1+1,\ell_2+1}]\le21L^2c_w^2m_4+210K^2m_4+27K^2c_s.$$

**Assessment.**
- *Truth:* true. Apply thm 4 pointwise with $\mu=\mu(z)=E[g_{\ell_2+1}-g_{\ell_2}\mid z]$, which satisfies $|\mu|\le c_w2^{-\ell_2}$. The three terms are bounded as follows:
  - $E[\max(\cdot)^4]\le96m_4/M^2$;
  - $E(\bar\delta-\mu)^4\le12c_s2^{-4\ell_2}/M^2$;
  - $E(\bar\delta_1-\bar\delta_2)^4\le48c_s2^{-4\ell_2}/M^2$.

  Combining with Cauchy–Schwarz gives $4^{\ell_1+\ell_2}E\Delta^2\le4.5L^2c_w^2m_4+38.2K^2\sqrt{m_4c_s}$, which is at most the stated RHS by AM–GM.
- *Numerics:* maximum ratio $6.7\cdot10^{-4}$, both over 3000 random models and under hill-climbing (`t08_sde_mimc.out`, `t08b_hillclimb.out`). The constant is extremely loose but valid.
- *Vacuity:* none; example of 8 with $f=\sin$, $K=L=1$, $c_w=\tfrac12$.
- *Hypotheses:* `hw` is uniform in $z$, stronger than needed.
- *Junk values:* none.
- *Fidelity:* matches the paper's $V=O(2^{-2\ell_1-2\ell_2})$.
